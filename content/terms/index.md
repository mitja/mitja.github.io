## Noctulog {#noctulog}

App for iPhone, website [noctulog.com](https://noctulog.com/en/).

### Not a medical device

Noctulog is a diary for sleep sounds. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose anything and does not replace a medical examination or advice. Automatically detected moments are based on measured loudness and the device's sound recognition, and they may be wrong or incomplete. This applies, for example, to "Snoring", "Talking", "Coughing" or "Silence after snoring". Do not make health decisions on this basis alone. If you have symptoms or suspect a sleep disorder, please see a doctor.

### Recording other people

You are responsible for recording only people who have agreed to it. Recording someone's private spoken words without consent may be a criminal offence (in Germany under Section 201 of the Criminal Code).

### Your data

Recordings and analyses are stored only on your device. Full recordings are excluded from iCloud and device backups. You are responsible for backing them up, for example by exporting them. Recordings that have been deleted or reduced by the retention settings cannot be restored. I do not guarantee that every night is recorded completely, for example if the battery runs out, storage is full or the operating system ends the app. See the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/#apps-noctulog) for details.

### Purchases

- **Free:** Noctulog can be used for free without a time limit, and you can record as often as you like. During your first seven nights (30 minutes or longer) every feature is unlocked. After that you can view and listen to the most recent night.
- **Older nights** are not deleted but locked until you buy Plus. Your automatic deletion settings still apply.
- **"Noctulog Plus"** unlocks all nights, the calendar, insights and export. You can share it via Family Sharing.
- **"Plus Yearly"** is a yearly subscription. It renews automatically for another year unless you cancel it in your Apple Account settings at least 24 hours before it ends. When it ends, older nights are locked again but not deleted.
- **"Plus Lifetime"** is a one-time purchase without a subscription. You pay once, and it does not renew.
- **On other devices** with the same Apple ID, you can use your purchases via "Restore Purchases".

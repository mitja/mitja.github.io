Hi, I am Mitja Martini. By day I work as a Cloud Services Solution Designer at T-Systems International GmbH. On the side I give trainings and create software with AI elements.

Currently, I am mostly creating software that helps automate IT operations with coding agent. I have already published a few open source projects and hopefully launch my first paid app, soon.

I want to use AI responsibly, safely, and in ways that creates value. I believe in learning by doing and am probably too much into it, right now. In the spare time left, I enjoy time with my family (we have a daughter), our poodle and our cats. I like watersports, mainly SUP and  windsurfing and try to stay fit with jogging and in my home gym.

If you want to talk or work with me, send an email to [hi@mitjamartini.com](mailto:hi@mitjamartini.com), and I get back to you.

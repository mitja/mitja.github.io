Hi, I am Mitja Martini. By day I work as a Cloud Services Solution Designer at T-Systems International GmbH. On the side I work in Berlin as a trainer and coach, with three focus areas: AI engineering, agentic DevOps and platform engineering.

I have also built and run small apps for years. In 2025 I started working with coding agents, and by early 2026 I could build a small SaaS app in a day or two. Shipping it was the slow part: auth, DNS, email, payments, monitoring, deployment. Kubernetes turned out to be a good place to run all of that, and Gardener automates Kubernetes itself. That is where [paasbox](https://paasbox.com) came from, a Hetzner Cloud landscape from one command, as well as the [Cloud Viewer](https://cloudviewer.app) app for Hetzner servers and [Mitjas Relay](/en/apps/relay/).

My goal is to use AI responsibly, safely, and in ways that create measurable value. I believe in learning by doing: every training session ends with something you built, and every coaching works on your real project.

You can reach me at [hi@mitjamartini.com](mailto:hi@mitjamartini.com). The provider details are on the [imprint](/en/imprint/) page.

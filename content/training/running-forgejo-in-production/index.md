## Workshops

1. **The migration dry run**
2. **Actions and runners**
3. **Operating it** — backup, restore, upgrades, SSO/LDAP, object storage
4. **Scaling and cost**
5. **The real migration** — large repositories, LFS, users, permissions, mirroring
6. **A coding agent in CI, on your own infrastructure**
7. **The private monorepo with public parts**
8. **Surviving the open internet** — bots, scrapers and abuse
9. **Scheduled work** — dependency updates, scanning and SBOMs

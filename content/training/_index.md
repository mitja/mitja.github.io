## How it works

- **Browser workspace.** Each session opens an editor and terminals in the browser ([Educates](https://docs.educates.dev)), with the tools installed and the lab environment already running. Nothing touches your laptop.
- **Pilot phase.** The first sessions run on my own servers, so seats are limited. During the pilot they are free.
- **Later.** Sessions will be paid; the price is on the page before you book.
- **Pre-registration** is an email to me. I write back when the first sessions are scheduled.

## Sessions

- **Week 0** — setup, self-paced, about 90 min
- **Week 1:** from spec to a deployed first feature (live); the core product (homework)
- **Week 2:** an AI feature as production software (live); brand, legal pages, email, an eval set (homework)
- **Week 3:** payments with Polar, your own server, launch (live); go live (homework)
- **Demo day** after week 3

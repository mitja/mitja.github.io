## Labs

All eight labs are written and were walked through end to end on 2026-10-03; none has run for participants yet.

1. The factory model and a reproducible repository (75 min)
2. The work packet (90 min)
3. The harness: sensors and hooks (90 min)
4. Sandboxes and security (90 min)
5. Verification (85 min)
6. Orchestration (110 min)
7. Operations: metrics and budgets (90 min)
8. Autonomy and the capstone (120 min): five packets go through the factory, and one merges without review by your own policy

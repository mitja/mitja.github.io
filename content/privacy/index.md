## Websites

* **Server log files.** When you open a page, your browser necessarily sends data that the web server logs: date and time, the page requested, the status code, the amount of data transferred, the referrer URL, the browser identification (type, version, operating system) and the IP address. I use this data to deliver the services, find errors and fend off attacks, and do not combine it with other data. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in secure, working websites (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). On my servers at Hetzner the logs are kept for at most 90 days and then deleted; for the pages on GitHub Pages, GitHub keeps logs under its own rules.
* **TLS encryption.** All pages are served encrypted (HTTPS) only, so third parties cannot read the data transferred.
* **Reach measurement with Umami.** On mitjamartini.com I measure how often which pages are visited with Umami, self-hosted open-source software on my server at Hetzner. Umami sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and does not store your IP address; it records the page visited, the referrer URL, browser, operating system, device type, screen size and the country derived from the IP address. I only look at aggregated numbers and pass nothing to third parties. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in improving the content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can prevent the measurement by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser or blocking scripts from umami.mitja.app.
* **YouTube videos.** Some blog posts embed videos from YouTube (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). When you open such a page, your browser connects to YouTube's servers; YouTube learns which page you opened and can link this to your YouTube account if you are signed in. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in an appealing presentation of the content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details: https://policies.google.com/privacy
* **Language and colour scheme.** If you choose a language or a colour scheme, your browser remembers it in local storage (e.g. `mm-lang`). These values never leave your browser. This is strictly necessary for the function you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
* **No forms.** mitjamartini.com and promptflower.com have no forms and no comments. Links to other websites only take effect when you click them; from then on, that website's privacy policy applies.

## Apps: Prompt Flower

Prompt Flower is my teleprompter app for iPhone (and later Android) whose text follows your voice. The app is built so that your content stays on your device. I run no server for the app, you need no account, and I receive no data about you or how you use it. The website sections above (server log files, Umami, YouTube) do not apply to the app.

* **On-device speech recognition.** To let the text follow your voice, the app listens to the microphone. Recognition runs entirely on your device, using iOS speech recognition in on-device-only mode. The app never sends audio or text to me, to Apple or to anyone else; if on-device recognition is not available, the text scrolls at a fixed pace instead. Audio and recognised text are used only for following your voice and are not stored, unless you choose to record.
* **Microphone, camera, Photos.** The app uses the microphone and camera only after you allow it in iOS, and only while you follow a script or record. Recordings (video or audio) and your scripts are stored locally on your device. They reach the Photos app or other apps only when you choose "Save to Photos" or "Share". Whether your device data is backed up to iCloud is your choice in iOS Settings; Apple's privacy policy applies to that.
* **iOS language packs.** For on-device recognition, iOS may download a language pack from Apple once. iOS does this itself, not the app; Apple's privacy policy applies (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
* **Remote control over Wi-Fi.** When you open "Remote", the app starts a small web server on your local network and shows a QR code containing an access key. A second device on your Wi-Fi can then control the prompter in its browser. Only control commands, the prompter's state (script title, position, status) and a short excerpt around the current position (a few words before and after) are exchanged – no audio, no video, not the whole script – and only between your devices on the local network, not over the internet. iOS asks for local network access for this. "Stop" disconnects all devices and invalidates the key.
* **Purchases.** You buy Prompt Flower Pro through the App Store. Apple handles the purchase, the payment and subscription management and processes your data for this as an independent controller (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). I receive no payment details and no personal data about your purchase, only aggregated sales reports from Apple without names. The app checks on your device, through Apple's App Store framework, whether Pro is active. The free daily minutes are also counted on the device only.
* **No analytics, no tracking, no ads.** The app contains no third-party analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting software, sends nothing automatically and uses no advertising identifier. If you allow iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides me with anonymised crash reports and usage statistics; you control this in iOS Settings under "Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements".
* **Crash reports only by e-mail, if you want.** If the app crashes, iOS leaves a technical report (MetricKit) in the app's storage. On the next launch the app asks whether you want to e-mail it to me, shows you its content first and sends nothing unless you send the e-mail yourself (only app version, build, iOS version and device model are included). "Don't Send" deletes the report; you can turn the question off under Settings › About › Crash Reports. The section "E-mail contact" applies to the e-mail.
* **Storage on your device.** Scripts, recordings (with their subtitle and marker data) and settings are kept in the app's storage on your device. You delete them in the app or, completely, by deleting the app. The app only reads and stores on your device what is strictly necessary for the functions you use (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
* **Support.** If you contact me via "Support" in the app, it opens an e-mail to support@mitjamartini.com with the subject "Prompt Flower <version>"; the section "E-mail contact" applies.

## Your rights

Regarding the personal data concerning you, you have the right to:

* **access** the stored data, its origin, recipients and the purpose of processing (Art. 15 GDPR),
* **rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
* **erasure** (Art. 17 GDPR) and **restriction of processing** (Art. 18 GDPR),
* **notification**: if I rectify, erase or restrict data, I inform the recipients I disclosed it to (Art. 19 GDPR),
* **data portability**: data I process automatically on the basis of your consent or a contract, in a machine-readable format (Art. 20 GDPR),
* **withdraw consent** with effect for the future; the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal is not affected (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

**Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR):** Where I process data on the basis of my legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. I will then stop, unless there are compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

An informal e-mail to privacy@mitjamartini.com is enough for any of these.

## How long I keep data

Where no period is given above: I keep data only as long as I need it for its purpose. Data based on your consent is deleted when you withdraw it. Data I must keep by law (e.g. up to 6 or 10 years under commercial and tax law) is deleted when those periods end. Data processed on the basis of my legitimate interest is deleted at the latest when you object successfully.

## Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for me is the [Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information](https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de/).

## No automated decisions

I do not make automated decisions, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
